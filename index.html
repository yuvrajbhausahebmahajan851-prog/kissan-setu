Sample data

Capacity, truck loads, rates and mandi prices are sample figures. In use, each hub operator and truck coordinator updates their own numbers. Accounts, bookings and payment records are saved only on this device, and the login is a demo without a server. UPI IDs are sample IDs: the hub operator confirms payment on receipt. 1 quintal = 100 kg; 10 quintals = 1 tonne. Distances are approximate road kilometres.
